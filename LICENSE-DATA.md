## Third-party inputs

- MOD13Q1 and MOD11A2 are products of the NASA LP DAAC and are in the public

  script that extracts them and the derived point tables.
- The river centreline is derived from OpenStreetMap data,

  (ODbL 1.0): <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>.
- The annual ecological water-transfer volumes were transcribed from public
  announcements of the Tarim River Basin Authority and are redistributed here
  as a factual table with attribution.
